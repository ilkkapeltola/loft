Where the dining table goes

At the balcony glass next to the kitchen (shown): a 0.8 × 1.4 m table for four, its short end at the window, two chairs on each side. The kitchen is a step away and the table gets the best daylight in the flat. The west chairs leave about 0.9 m to the kitchen front, and the east chairs about 0.9 m to the back of the sofa. The bay behind the wardrobes, where the table stood before, is now behind the L sofa's second leg and stays open.

Check before building

  • Things still to place: a folding drying rack, which lives in the cleaning cupboard and opens in the bathroom; the recycling bins under the sink; and the router and network box, in a coat cabinet with a power socket. A robot vacuum dock fits in the cleaning cupboard's base.
  • The kitchen goes on the southern kitchen reservation (KT-varaus) on the west wall, as in version 3. Confirm with EKE that its water and drain connections are planned there.
  • The bedroom is open above the wardrobes, from 3.4 m to the 4 m ceiling, so some light and sound come over the top. Fixing points in the wardrobe top let a glass or oak strip close it later.
  • The bathroom door opens out into the passage, hinged at its south end; open, it folds back 7 cm clear of the cabinet south of it. The bedroom door opens into the bedroom and parks across the cleaning cupboard's fixed panel, 10 cm in front of the wardrobes; the wardrobe doors east of it swing clear.
  • Dimensions are read off A21's 1:100 sales plan: 7.30 × 9.70 m inside, about 71 m² against the 68.5 m² listed, so expect up to 10–15 cm less in places.
  • A21 is on the 4th floor, two floors lower than A33.
  • "Evening, indirect" shows hidden warm-white LED lighting, each part on its own switch. The strips are drawn as soft area lights, so you can see where the glow lands. Light bouncing on from the lit ceiling isn't calculated: a warm fill stands in for it, so treat the overall brightness as indicative.
