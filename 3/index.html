Check before building

  • Dimensions are read off A21's 1:100 sales plan: 7.30 × 9.70 m inside, with the bathroom 2.34 m wide from 2.53 to 6.13 m back from the north wall. That gives about 71 m², against the 68.5 m² listed, so expect up to 10–15 cm less in places. The bedroom and the living room have the least slack.
  • Two facing sofas, as in version 1, don't fit. The living room here is 3.8 m deep rather than 5.6 m, and the stair lands in its corner. One 3-seater facing the TV keeps the walk from the stair and the passage to the balcony door clear.
  • The bedroom is 3.13 × 3.5 m: the whole parvi block moved 50 cm towards the balcony to make it bigger, at the cost of living-room depth. A 180 bed fits with 65 cm each side and 1.3 m at the foot. The headboard stands in front of the window, so it's kept low, under the 0.9 m sill. A 160 bed gives 85 cm each side.
  • The kitchen goes in the southern kitchen reservation (KT-varaus) on the west wall. Confirm with EKE that the water and drain connections are planned there and not only in the northern one.
  • The bedroom is open above the wardrobes, so light and sound come over the parvi railing. The curtains close it from inside the bedroom; the oak wall to the ceiling is the permanent alternative.
  • The parvi floor is at 2.40 m: 2.28 m below and 1.60 m above, the legal maximum before it counts as floor area. The stair has 12 risers of 20 cm. The doors under it, including the bathroom door, are 2.05 m tall. Anything carried in from the front door goes down the passage under the parvi, so it has to stay under 2.28 m. The tall eteinen is the place to turn a sofa upright.
  • A21 is on the 4th floor, two floors lower than A33.
  • "Evening, indirect" shows hidden warm-white LED lighting, each part on its own switch. The strips are drawn as soft area lights, so you can see where the glow lands. Light bouncing on from the lit ceiling isn't calculated: a warm fill stands in for it, so treat the overall brightness as indicative.
