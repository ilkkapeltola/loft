Artus A33 Kitchen Behind Coats

A new start. Step in from the front door and the coat wardrobes are straight away on your left: one dark-wood block, out to the same front line as new cabinets on the bathroom wall. The kitchen is built behind them: two matt anthracite runs facing each other, one on the wardrobes' back and one on the bathroom's shaft, open to a hall by the French balcony. The bedroom takes the north-west corner the way A21 /9 does it: a concrete wall with a hidden flush door, the bed's headboard on the outer wall, and tall wardrobes as its south wall. The rest of the flat is left empty for now. No parvi; 4 m everywhere.

Check before building

  • The palette is the dark "Rouhea" one used in versions 6 and 12: raw grey concrete walls, ceiling and floor, dark brown wood for all the joinery, a matt anthracite kitchen with a stainless sink, black steel and a moss-green jute rug. The bathroom box stays white.
  • The kitchen isn't in EKE's kitchen reservation, but it backs onto the bathroom's north shaft, so the water, the drain and the hood duct have the shortest possible run. Check with EKE that the shaft can take them.
  • The bedroom wall stands just west of the French balcony, so the balcony door and its light belong to the hall, not the bedroom. Moving the wall east past it would leave the hall too narrow to reach the kitchen.
  • The bedroom is 2.83 m wide, so the wall stands 15 cm clear of the French balcony window. With the bed against the window there is about 47 cm on each side of a 180 bed: enough for slim nightstands, but tight to walk past. A 160 bed would give about 57 cm each side.
  • The entry is 1.15 m wide. The front door opens outwards, so the full width stays clear.
  • Dimensions come from A33's 1:100 sales plan, good to about 10 cm.
  • "Evening, indirect" shows hidden warm-white LED lighting, each part on its own switch. The strips are drawn as soft area lights, so you can see where the glow lands. Light bouncing on from the lit ceiling isn't calculated: a warm fill stands in for it, so treat the overall brightness as indicative.
