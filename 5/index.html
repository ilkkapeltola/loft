Check before building

  • The wardrobes stop at 2.4 m, so light and sound carry over them. The curtain blocks sight and light at the doorway, not sound. "Wardrobes to the ceiling" shows them carried up to 4 m: a properly closed bedroom and a lot of ladder-height storage, but the middle of the flat loses its north light.
  • Clothes storage is 3 m of wardrobes in the eteinen and 3.85 m in the bedroom. There's no separate store in this version; the cleaning gear and suitcases need a place, in the cellar unit or the eteinen's top cupboards.
  • The desk faces the oak wardrobe back, not a window. The daylight comes from the south glazing, 4–6 m behind you, and over the wardrobes from the north.
  • The kitchen is on the southern kitchen reservation (KT-varaus) on the east wall. Confirm with EKE that the water and drain connections are planned there too. Dimensions come from A33's 1:100 sales plan, good to about 10 cm.
  • "Evening, indirect" shows hidden warm-white LED lighting, each part on its own switch. The strips are drawn as soft area lights, so you can see where the glow lands. Light bouncing on from the lit ceiling isn't calculated: a warm fill stands in for it, so treat the overall brightness as indicative.
