Check before building

  • The bedroom has a door to the eteinen but is open to the passage and the living room, for sound and evening light. You can see into it from the passage, not from the front door. A ceiling curtain track across the opening, where version 3 has its curtains, is a cheap fix if it bothers you.
  • Fixed now so step 2 doesn't undo finished work: run the floor finish under the whole core; put empty conduit for the parvi's lights, sockets and desk into the east wall and the bathroom face before they're finished; and have the parvi's structural design done now, so the bearing points are known. The bathroom box is EKE's: confirm whether the deck can bear on it.
  • The wardrobes are fixed to the east wall and the floor; the west end stands free, so make the carcass rigid. In step 2 the parvi deck sits 2 cm above them. Measure the real ceiling height before building either step: the deck height follows from it (at most 1.60 m above the deck).
  • No store in step 1: the vacuum and cleaning gear need a place, in the eteinen's top cupboards or the cellar unit, until step 2.
  • Roughly what moves to step 2, by version 1's estimate scaled to the smaller parvi: parvi €14k, stair €7k, railings €5k, the oak faces, doors and store fittings about €5k. About €30k, less the wardrobes (≈ €4k) built now.
  • Dimensions are read off A21's 1:100 sales plan: 7.30 × 9.70 m inside, about 71 m² against the 68.5 m² listed, so expect up to 10–15 cm less in places. The kitchen goes in the southern kitchen reservation (KT-varaus) on the west wall: confirm with EKE that the water and drain connections are planned there.
  • A21 is on the 4th floor, two floors lower than A33.
  • "Evening, indirect" shows hidden warm-white LED lighting, each part on its own switch. The strips are drawn as soft area lights, so you can see where the glow lands. Light bouncing on from the lit ceiling isn't calculated: a warm fill stands in for it, so treat the overall brightness as indicative.
