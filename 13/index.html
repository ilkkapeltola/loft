Artus A33 Horizon

A clean-slate design built on one rule: nothing built rises above 2.4 m except a wall of glass. The bedroom is a dark-timber box in the north-west corner with glass up to the ceiling. Coats go into 1.9 m of timber wardrobes immediately left of the front door; the kitchen follows them, with an island that ends in an oak table for four. Then the room opens: a 7 m concrete wall washed with light, one low bench along it, a 65″ picture TV on the wall and a low green corner sofa facing it. The study is its own world behind a 4 m steel-and-glass screen in the south-east corner, by the window.

The idea

  • One horizon. Everything built is a dark-timber box or cabinet exactly 2.4 m high. Above that line there is only concrete, glass and light, so the ceiling runs unbroken from wall to wall and the full 4 m is read everywhere. No joinery at 3.4 m that “almost” reaches: either clearly low, or nothing.
  • One long wall. The longest concrete wall is washed with warm light from its foot to the ceiling. One thing hangs on it: a 65″ picture TV (Samsung The Frame) opposite the sofa, flush to the concrete, showing art when it is off. The wash stops 20 cm either side of it, so the picture sits in a calm, unlit field and nothing glares on the screen.
  • One long line. Under that wall runs a single low bench, 40 cm high. The wall-wash light, the TV's connection box, hi-fi and storage are inside it; nothing stands on it.
  • Emptiness on purpose. You arrive onto bare floor with the lit wall ahead; the seating starts further in. The sofa is the 290 × 230 cm green corduroy corner sofa, low and square, facing the wall with its short leg along the window. Between it and the glass the floor is left empty.
  • Soft against hard. 4 m sheer linen on a ceiling track covers the whole window wall when drawn, and is lit from the track at night: the black glass becomes a glowing veil. With the big wool rug it also takes the echo out of a concrete room, which is half of what “calm” means.
  • Mesh and wash don't mix, so they don't meet. The washed wall stays plain. The backlit steel mesh belongs to the study, where it is a tool wall behind 4 m of black steel glazing: seen from outside, the study glows like a lantern.
  • The island ends in a table. No dining table. The kitchen island's stone block carries on as a thick oak slab at the same height: two eat there daily, four face to face when needed, and all four stools live under the slab. It takes no floor of its own.
  • Work by the glass, cook by the door. Here the study gets the daylight corner south of the bathroom: side light, the view, a door to close. The kitchen sits by the entrance behind the coat wardrobes, where guests land anyway. (A21 /14 tries it the other way round.)

Check before building

  • The TV: a 65″ The Frame is 146 × 84 cm and hangs flush on its own wall mount. Its one thin cable runs to the connection box in the bench; on raw concrete that cable needs a chased groove or a slim painted conduit, to be decided with the builder.
  • The wall wash is a continuous LED grazer in the bench's rear edge, about 5 cm off the wall, in two runs with a gap behind the TV. Grazing light shows every flaw in the concrete: look at the wall with a torch held flat against it before committing.
  • Glass above the 2.4 m boxes is fixed and frameless. It keeps sound in but lets light through; the bedroom needs a blind on the track inside if you sleep while lights are on outside.
  • The steel-and-glass screen is 4 m high: fixed to floor and ceiling, laminated glass, sliding door hung from the 2.4 m transom.
  • The island has only the induction hob (recirculating downdraft, no hood), so it needs power in the floor but no drain. The sink and dishwasher stay by the shaft.
  • No ceiling lights in the living room at all: wall wash, veil, the picture, the study lantern and one reading lamp. Dimmable in scenes.
  • 2.4 m cabinets hold less than 3.4 m ones. Seasonal things go to the top shelves of the wardrobes and to the cellar storage.
  • Dimensions come from the 1:100 sales plan, good to about 10 cm.
